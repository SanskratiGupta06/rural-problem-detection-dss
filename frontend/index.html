The problem

Villages have problems. Decisions lack evidence.

Problems are scattered across paper registers and phone calls. Nobody can see which village needs what first.

📊 Village priority score Working

Weighted by category, built from field-survey severity (70%) and open complaints (30%). Higher means more serious.
